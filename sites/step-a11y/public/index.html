    Footwear for every activity, in one place

    STEP has been fitting shoes since 1978, starting from a single counter and growing into fourteen branches and a warehouse that ships nationwide. Across running, sneakers, boots, kids, and sport we carry 1,284 styles from twelve houses, including twelve colorways sold nowhere else in the country.

    Fit first, everything else second

    Every branch measures feet free of charge, and six branches run a treadmill gait analysis that takes about twenty minutes. There is no obligation to buy afterwards, and the specialist is not paid on commission — which is why the recommendation is as often the cheaper shoe as the expensive one.

    Buying online without the guesswork

    Product pages carry the same measurements our staff use in store: stack height, drop, weight on a size 42, and whether the model runs small or large based on aggregated exchange data rather than opinion. Standard delivery takes three to five business days, express arrives next day when ordered before 2 pm, and same-day pickup is available in every branch.

    Returns that do not require an argument

    Sixty days on full-price footwear, thirty on sale items, free either way. Drop the parcel at any branch or book a courier collection from your account. Refunds are processed within two business days of the parcel arriving, and exchanges ship as soon as the courier scans your return rather than waiting for it to land.

    Care, repair, and a longer second life

    Eight branches offer resoling, stitching, heel replacement, and eyelet repair, usually within five to seven business days. Custom insoles are fitted in a forty-five minute appointment, and club members at Sprint tier and above get a pair free once a year.

    Sustainability, measured rather than claimed

    Recycled content averages 62% across our own-label uppers, and every figure on a product page is the audited number from the supplier, not a marketing estimate. Our take-back scheme has collected 41,000 pairs since 2021, sorted for resale, donation, or granulation into playground surfacing.

    How to measure your foot

    1. Measure in the evening. Feet swell over the day, and a shoe fitted in the morning is often half a size too small by 6 pm.
    2. Wear the socks you plan to use with the shoe. A running sock and a wool winter sock differ by nearly half a size.
    3. Place a sheet of paper flat against a wall and stand on it with your heel touching the wall, weight even on both feet.
    4. Mark the tip of your longest toe — which is not always the big toe.
    5. Measure from the wall edge of the paper to the mark, in centimeters. Repeat for the other foot.
    6. Use the larger of the two numbers, add 8–10 mm for everyday shoes or 10–12 mm for running, and match it to the CM column in our chart.

    Still unsure? Any branch will measure both feet at no charge, with no appointment needed.
